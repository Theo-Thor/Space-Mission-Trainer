# Science and fiction

Chapter 1 is the introduction. It does not yet use a NASA dataset, and it should not be described as if it does.

## Fictional

- Selene Mission Preparation Facility
- The spacecraft and the boarding hatch
- The crew: the player, Riley Morgan, Dr. Maya Chen, Leo Voss, Nia Brooks, Val Ortiz, and Dr. Samir Patel
- Mission Control lines in this chapter
- The spacesuit readiness steps on screen
- The Moon picture in the briefing room, which is a drawn disc, not a mission map

None of these are NASA assets, flight hardware, or measurements.

## What the dialogue is allowed to say

The briefing and Dr. Patel's optional conversation stay qualitative:

- Some areas near the lunar poles stay in shadow.
- Those regions may preserve water ice. The mission is to investigate evidence, not to assume a discovery.
- Lighting can differ sharply across short distances near the pole, which matters for power and route planning.

No temperatures, depths, coordinates, or ice amounts are invented.

## Later data, not used here

A later chapter can connect player choices to real lunar data. Candidates, still unused:

- Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter south-pole illumination maps
- NASA Moon Trek / LRO layers for illumination and Earth visibility near the south pole

Until those sources are loaded and cited in the game, the Moon graphic remains an illustration.
